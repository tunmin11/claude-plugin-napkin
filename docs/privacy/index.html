Privacy policy

Napkin is designed to explain ideas, not to build a profile about you. This page describes what the Napkin project itself does and where the platform provider's privacy practices apply.

What Napkin collects

The Napkin project does not operate its own user accounts, analytics service, advertising network, or database. We do not independently collect or store the prompts, documents, or learning pages you use with the Napkin skill.

How your content is handled

Napkin runs within the AI platform where you use it. That platform processes your prompts and any content you choose to provide, under its own privacy policy, settings and terms. If a task uses a platform-provided web search or other external tool, the relevant query or request may also be processed by that provider.

Do not submit passwords, payment details, confidential business information, or sensitive personal information unless you understand and accept the platform's data-handling practices.

This website

This informational website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as server logs, to operate and secure its hosting service. We do not add advertising trackers or third-party analytics to this site. Please review GitHub's Privacy Statement for details about its services.

Third-party services

Links to GitHub, Anthropic, OpenAI, or other services take you to websites and services operated by those providers. Their own privacy policies govern their handling of your information.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Napkin's features or hosting arrangements change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised date.

Questions about this policy? Please use the support page.
